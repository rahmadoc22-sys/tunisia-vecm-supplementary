# Data Availability Statement

> The data analyzed in this study were constructed exclusively from official primary sources: the Institut National de la Statistique (INS, Tunisia) industrial production index and consumer price index as disseminated through the IMF's *International Financial Statistics* (IFS), the Banque Centrale de Tunisie (BCT) statistical tables (money-market rate and monetary aggregates), and the INS monthly conjuncture bulletin of June 2026. The public supplementary material — figures, result tables, and a data note documenting every source by retrieval code — is archived under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22650238 (latest version: 10.5281/zenodo.23036848), licensed CC-BY 4.0. The full replication package (raw retrievals, construction scripts, derived datasets, estimation code, and outputs) is available from the author and provided to reviewers and the editorial office on request under a proprietary license; commercial licensing available on request. Every observation in the analysis file can be traced to a named official series code or publication, as documented in the package data note. No proprietary or restricted-access data were used.
